# Ai2 and Hugging Face Expand Partnership, Unlocking 2 Petabytes for Open AI

## Summary
AI2 and Hugging Face have expanded their partnership, tripling AI2's Hub storage to ~2 petabytes and removing download rate limits. AI2 is now the most prolific publisher of open AI artifacts on the Hub, with 968+ models, 1,275+ datasets, and 50M+ downloads since spring 2024.

## How It Works
Hugging Face increased AI2's storage allocation from its previous level to approximately 2 petabytes and eliminated standard rate-limit restrictions on AI2's downloads. AI2's portfolio includes models, datasets, benchmarks (olmOCR-Bench is now an official leaderboard), and applications like MolmoAct 2, an open robotics model that achieved 37x inference speedup over v1 and ships with 720 hours of bimanual manipulation robot data.

## Why It Matters
The scale of AI2's output—more annual artifacts than any other tracked organization—has made it a strategic priority for Hugging Face's infrastructure. Practitioners working with large model checkpoints and datasets benefit directly from the removal of rate limits, which were previously a friction point for downloads. The deal signals that open-science publishing at scale is now a core positioning for the Hub, and AI2's incoming pipeline (multimodal models, tool-using agents, science-domain models backed by a $152M compute project) suggests continued growth in freely available research artifacts.
