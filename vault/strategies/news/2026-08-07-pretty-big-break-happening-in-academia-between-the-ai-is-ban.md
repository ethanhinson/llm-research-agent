# Pretty big break happening in academia between the “AI is banned for reviews” journals and the “AI is mandatory for reviews” journals. www.refine.ink/blog/economi...

## Summary
Refine, an AI-assisted technical verification tool, has been adopted by the American Economic Association and the Econometric Society for use in their publication workflows. The tool identifies errors in reasoning, calculation, and references to provide pre-publication audits of technical execution and exposition.

## How It Works
Refine scans manuscripts to catch errors in reasoning, calculation, and references that reviewers might miss. Journals use it primarily as a pre-publication audit, providing detailed feedback to authors before final publication. Editors may also deploy Refine's technical comments earlier in the refereeing process. The tool is offered free to journals within capacity limits.

## Why It Matters
As AI-generated analysis becomes easier to produce, verification of rigor and correctness has become a critical bottleneck in research quality. By automating detection of technical errors across reasoning, calculation, and citations, Refine offers journals a scalable mechanism to catch mistakes before publication, reducing the need for post-publication corrections and strengthening trust in the research record.
