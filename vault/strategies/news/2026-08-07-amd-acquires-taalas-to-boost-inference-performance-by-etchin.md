# AMD acquires Taalas to boost inference performance by etching models in silicon

## Summary
AMD has acquired Taalas, an AI chip startup, to enhance inference performance through custom silicon that embeds models directly into hardware. Early technical demonstrations show the resulting model-specific integrated circuits achieving throughput of up to 17,000 tokens per second.

## How It Works
Taalas develops model-specific integrated circuits that etch AI models into silicon, replacing generic compute with hardware optimized for particular model architectures. This approach moves inference workloads from software execution on general-purpose processors to dedicated silicon.

## Why It Matters
Custom silicon for inference addresses a key bottleneck in LLM deployment—the throughput and efficiency gap in serving models at scale. Hardware-specific integration can dramatically reduce latency and power consumption compared to general-purpose inference, making it attractive for production deployments where token-per-second throughput directly impacts cost and user experience.
