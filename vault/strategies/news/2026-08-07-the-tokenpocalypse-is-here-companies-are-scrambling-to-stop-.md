# The Tokenpocalypse Is Here: Companies Are Scrambling To Stop Spending So Much on AI

## Summary
Companies are experiencing high token consumption costs from AI systems, with Accenture's internal data revealing that inefficient workflows—particularly converting PDFs to images and then to markdown—are major drivers. The source suggests this "tokenpocalypse" reflects wasteful data handling practices rather than core engineering constraints.

## How It Works
According to leaked Accenture meeting audio, non-engineering staff are driving token consumption through inefficient document processing pipelines. Specifically, converting PDFs into image formats and then into markdown files is identified as a significant token consumer, indicating that intermediate representation steps multiply token usage unnecessarily.

## Why It Matters
As organizations scale AI deployment, token costs have become a visible operational burden. Understanding that wasteful preprocessing—not model use itself—is a primary cost driver suggests that practitioners can reduce expenses substantially through better data pipeline design, making AI projects more economically sustainable without waiting for model improvements.
