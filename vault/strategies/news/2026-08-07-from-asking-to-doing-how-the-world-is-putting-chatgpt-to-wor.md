# From asking to doing: How the world is putting ChatGPT to work

## Summary
OpenAI has published country-by-country ChatGPT usage data covering over 1 billion users, revealing a shift from exploratory question-asking toward productive task completion. Work-related use cases—writing, coding, analysis—now dominate, and adoption is spreading globally beyond early adopters to older age groups and underrepresented regions.

## How It Works
The data comes from OpenAI Signals, a public analytics hub covering individual ChatGPT accounts (Free, Go, Plus, Pro tiers). OpenAI categorizes messages by use type (practical guidance, writing, information-seeking, multimedia) and tracks adoption by country, region, and age cohort. At work, users are more than twice as likely to "do" (produce output or complete tasks) versus "ask" (seek information). Multimedia use has grown to 7.8% of messages globally following the April 2026 release of ChatGPT Images 2.0, with rates exceeding 10% in Brazil and Colombia.

## Why It Matters
The data indicates ChatGPT is shifting from a research and experimentation tool toward embedded professional and creative workflows. Policymakers and researchers can use these public statistics to understand real-world adoption patterns and inform regulation or investment. The narrowing adoption gap—with Latin America, Africa, and Oceania catching up to early adopters, and older users entering the user base in significant numbers—suggests the technology is becoming mainstream infrastructure rather than confined to tech-early-adopter populations.
