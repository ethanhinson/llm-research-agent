## How It Works
This workshop brings together researchers from academia and industry who work at the intersection of neuro-symbolic AI, mathematical reasoning, and intelligent agents. We focus on architectures that combine logical and probabilistic reasoning with deep learning, knowledge graphs, and large language models, with particular emphasis on formal and informal mathematical reasoning, world models for agents, and continuous/continual learning.
