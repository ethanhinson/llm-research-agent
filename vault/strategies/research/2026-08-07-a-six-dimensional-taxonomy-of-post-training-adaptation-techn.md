# A Six-Dimensional Taxonomy of Post-Training Adaptation Techniques with Applications in AI Governance

## Summary
This survey synthesizes fragmented post-training adaptation literature into a unified six-dimensional taxonomy covering mechanism, goal, data requirement, persistence, structural scope, and model type. It distinguishes commonly conflated techniques and maps their relationships across traditional ML through multimodal LLMs, offering a shared vocabulary for technical documentation, model tracking, and governance.

## How It Works
The taxonomy organizes post-training adaptation techniques—including fine-tuning, retrieval augmentation, alignment, model editing, unlearning, and calibration—along six dimensions. It traces how adaptation strategies evolve across model classes and identifies relationships among techniques such as inheritance, supersession, hybridization, and layered deployment stacks.

## Why It Matters
The fragmented nature of post-training literature makes it difficult to compare methods or track how models are modified in practice. A unified taxonomy supports practitioners in model documentation, change tracking, and governance analysis, while identifying open research gaps in evaluation, reproducibility, persistent inference-time adaptation, and unlearning at scale.
