# [2306.13549] A Survey on Multimodal Large Language Models

## Summary
A comprehensive survey of Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs) that leverage LLMs as a foundation to process multiple modalities. The work traces recent progress in MLLM development, noting emergent capabilities like image-to-story generation and OCR-free reasoning that distinguish these systems from traditional multimodal approaches.

## How It Works
The survey organizes MLLMs across several dimensions: basic formulation and architecture, training strategies and datasets, and evaluation methods. It covers extensions enabling greater granularity, additional modalities, multilingual support, and diverse scenarios. The work also addresses multimodal hallucination and extended reasoning techniques including Multimodal In-Context Learning (M-ICL), Multimodal Chain-of-Thought (M-CoT), and LLM-Aided Visual Reasoning (LAVR).

## Why It Matters
Practitioners benefit from a structured overview of the MLLM landscape as the field rapidly expands and attracts significant academic and industry effort. The survey identifies core architectural and training patterns while cataloging both capabilities and failure modes (hallucination), providing grounded reference points for building or evaluating systems competing with models like GPT-4V.
