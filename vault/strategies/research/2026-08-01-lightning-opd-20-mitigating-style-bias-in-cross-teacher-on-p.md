## How It Works
On-policy distillation (OPD) provides dense token-level supervision from a teacher, but its effectiveness can depend on teacher consistency, meaning that the model providing OPD supervision should also have generated the demonstrations used to train the supervised fine-tuning (SFT) reference. However, this condition is frequently violated in practice when SFT data have mixed or unknown provenance or when different models are preferred for SFT data generation and subsequent distillation.
