# Choose a design pattern for your agentic AI system

## Summary
The multi-agent loop agent pattern repeatedly executes a sequence of specialized subagents until a specific termination condition is met.

## How It Works
The multi-agent loop agent pattern repeatedly executes a sequence of specialized subagents until a specific termination condition is met. This pattern uses a loop workflow agent that, like other workflow agents, operates on predefined logic without consulting an AI model for orchestration. After all of the subagents complete their tasks, the loop agent evaluates whether an exit condition is met. The condition can be a maximum number of iterations or a custom state.
