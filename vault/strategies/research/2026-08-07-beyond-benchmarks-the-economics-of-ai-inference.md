## Summary

## How It Works
Cost is the ultimate benchmark that determines whether a technical solution can be scaled. We use the total test set cost derived in Sections 3 and 4 as the core unit cost metric, which unifies hardware, energy, and time into quantifiable financial data. The “cost” here is not a variable independent of performance but a relative computational expense calculated based on performance thresholds, representing the economic efficiency of resource utilization.
