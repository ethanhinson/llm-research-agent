## How It Works
Video-MME-v2 introduces a next-generation benchmark for evaluating multimodal video understanding models. Built over 3,300 human-hours with 12 annotators and 50 reviewers (up to 5 QA rounds per question), it structures video comprehension into a tri-level hierarchy and replaces per-question accuracy with group-based non-linear evaluation that penalizes guessing.
