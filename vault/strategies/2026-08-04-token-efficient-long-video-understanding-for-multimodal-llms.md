## How It Works
Understanding long videos with VLMs presents significant challenges in both accuracy and efficiency. Previous approaches have employed long-context language models trained on short-context video data to enable long video comprehension . However, these methods lack sufficient long video training data and incur high computational costs during both training and inference as the number of frames increases.
