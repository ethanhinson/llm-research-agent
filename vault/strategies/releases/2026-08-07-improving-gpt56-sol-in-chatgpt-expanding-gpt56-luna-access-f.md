# Improving GPT‑5.6 Sol in ChatGPT, expanding GPT‑5.6 Luna access for free users

## Summary
OpenAI has updated GPT-5.6 Sol for Plus/Pro users with improved factual accuracy and a new thought-level slider, while expanding GPT-5.6 Luna access to free users with unlimited text chats and a Think button for deeper reasoning. The updates aim to make everyday conversations more reliable and direct while broadening model availability.

## How It Works
GPT-5.6 Sol has been retrained to better use retrieved sources, reducing factual errors by approximately 68% on financial, medical, and legal prompts compared to GPT-5.5 Instant. For Plus/Pro users, a slider control lets them choose how much reasoning effort the model applies per response. Free users now get GPT-5.6 Luna as the default model with unlimited text chats, plus a Think button to access higher reasoning when needed. The model tone and behavior are now more consistent between instant and deeper-reasoning modes.

## Why It Matters
With 1 billion weekly ChatGPT users relying on the platform for diverse tasks from quick questions to complex decisions, improving factual reliability and expanding free access directly impacts how broadly AI assistance reaches users. The thought-level slider gives paid users explicit control over inference compute trade-offs, while free users gain practical access to more capable reasoning without rate limits—reducing barriers to using AI for planning, research, and multi-step problems.
