# Meta AI Sweeps Five STEM Olympiads With Perfect Scores and Zero Tools

## Summary
Meta AI's models achieved perfect scores on the Asian Physics Olympiad (APhO, 30/30) and International Physics Olympiad (IPhO), plus gold medals at the International Mathematical Olympiad (IMO), International Chemistry Olympiad (IChO), and Romanian Masters of Mathematics (RMM)—all without tools, calculators, code, or search. The result demonstrates that LLMs can now perform genuine multi-step reasoning on novel, proof-based problems rather than pattern matching.

## How It Works
The source text does not describe the model architecture or reasoning approach. It only specifies that the models completed Olympiad competitions under the constraint of zero tool use, meaning they produced full written proofs and derivations using internal reasoning alone, matching the conditions human contestants face.

## Why It Matters
Olympiad problems require creativity, logical reasoning, and first-principles derivation under pressure—tasks historically considered beyond LLM capability. Perfect and gold-medal scores at multiple elite competitions, achieved without external aids, signal a qualitative shift in what these systems can do with reasoning-heavy tasks. The source notes that IMO is now considered a saturated benchmark, suggesting the field must develop harder evaluation standards to measure further progress.
