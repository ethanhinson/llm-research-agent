# Artificial Analysis Rebuilds Its Image Arena to Rank Models by Real Work

## Summary
Artificial Analysis rebuilt its Text to Image Arena to rank models across 10 real-world use cases and 9 capability dimensions instead of a single overall score. The new structure reveals that GPT Image 2 dominates overall but that cheaper alternatives like Nano Banana 2 excel in specific workloads like UI/UX and text rendering.

## How It Works
Models are evaluated through blind pairwise human preference votes using an Elo rating system. Prompts are sampled evenly across all use case and capability combinations to weight each equally. Judging hints direct voter attention to what matters for each prompt's specific use case. Prompts are refreshed monthly from anonymized real-user data and retired when they stop discriminating between models to prevent leaderboard overfitting.

## Why It Matters
A practitioner choosing an image model now gets use-case-specific guidance rather than a single ranking, making it easier to optimize cost-quality tradeoffs for their particular work. For instance, a product team can see that Nano Banana 2 matches GPT Image 2 on UI/UX at one-third the cost, while a different studio might benefit from Reve 2.1's 4K layout strengths. The taxonomy also exposes shared weaknesses—physics is the weakest capability across all top models—surfacing where the field needs improvement.
