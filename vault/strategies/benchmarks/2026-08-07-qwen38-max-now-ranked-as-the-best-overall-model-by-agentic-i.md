# Qwen3.8 Max now ranked as the best overall model by agentic index

## Summary
The Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index v4.1.1 is a comprehensive benchmark suite that evaluates AI models across multiple dimensions including reasoning, cost, speed, and agentic capability. Qwen3.8 Max is reported to rank as the best overall model according to this index.

## How It Works
The Intelligence Index aggregates performance across nine distinct evaluations: GDPval-AA v2, τ³-Banking, Terminal-Bench v2.1, SciCode, Humanity's Last Exam, GPQA Diamond, CritPt, AA-Omniscience, and AA-LCR. Models are scored on intelligence metrics while also being tracked for cost per task, time per task, and output tokens consumed. The index includes specialized benchmarks such as AA-Briefcase for agentic knowledge work (testing agents on business workflows requiring deliverables like spreadsheets and presentations) and AA-Omniscience for knowledge reliability and hallucination detection (scoring from -100 to 100 based on correct answers, penalizing hallucinations).

## Why It Matters
Practitioners should care because the index provides a systematic, multi-faceted evaluation framework that goes beyond raw capability scores to include real-world operational factors like cost and latency per task. The inclusion of agentic benchmarks and hallucination penalties makes it relevant for production deployment decisions where not only intelligence but also reliability and economic efficiency matter. The benchmark's transparency about methodology and comprehensive model coverage (595 models tracked) makes it a practical reference for model selection in different use cases.
