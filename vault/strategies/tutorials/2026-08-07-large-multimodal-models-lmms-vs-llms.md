## How It Works
These can be understood as more advanced versions of large language models (LLMs) that can work with text and also on diverse data types. Additionally, multimodal language model outputs are designed to be textual, visual, auditory, and so on.

Multimodal language models are considered the next step toward achieving artificial general intelligence.
