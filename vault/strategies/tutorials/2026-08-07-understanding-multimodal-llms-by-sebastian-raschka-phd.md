## How It Works
What are multimodal LLMs? As hinted at in the introduction, multimodal LLMs are large language models capable of processing multiple types of inputs, where each "modality" refers to a specific type of data—such as text (like in traditional LLMs), sound, images, videos, and more. For simplicity, we will primarily focus on the image modality alongside text inputs.
