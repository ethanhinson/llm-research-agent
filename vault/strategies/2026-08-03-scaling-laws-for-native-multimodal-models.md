## How It Works
Fur-thermore, we observe that native multimodal models fol-low scaling laws similar to those of LLMs , albeit with slight variations in scaling coefficients across modalities and datasets. Our results suggest that model parameters and training tokens should be scaled roughly equally for optimal performance.
